Web Firmware Updater

Module/Desktop Select

1) Select your device and follow the Instructions below to put your device into enter the Bootloader (DFU Mode) for programming.


2) Press Connect and select your device in the browser popup. It should have a generic name like "DFU in FS Mode" or "STM32 BOOTLOADER"

3) Press Update Firmware to upload the firmware to the module.

Instructions / Changelog

Requirements / Notes

A Supported browser is required—
Chrome/Chromium (v61+), Edge (v79+), or Opera (v117+).

Contact Intellijel Support if you have any issues, please include a screenshot of the problem, as well as your OS and browser version.

Updater based on webdfu. You can see our source on Github.

Windows

On Windows an appropriate WinUSB/libusb driver must first be installed. This can be done manually with programs such as Zadig or automatically (sometimes...) with WCID.

Legacy Devices

Legacy Devices can be updated with our old firmware update tool—
Metropolis, Mutamix, Plog, Polaris USB ISP, µStep, µScale II.

Available on our Support page.
