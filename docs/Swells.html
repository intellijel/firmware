Entering Bootloader

  1. Turn off power to your modular system.
  2. Connect a USB cable from the rear of the module to your computer.
  3. Hold down the BOOT button on the rear of the module and turn on power to your modular system. Note: The LEDs will not light up when in bootloader mode.
