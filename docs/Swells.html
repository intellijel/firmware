Swells

Entering Bootloader

  1. Turn off power to your modular system.
  2. Connect a USB cable from the rear of the module to your computer.
  3. Hold down the BOOT button on the rear of the module and turn on power to your modular system. Note: The LEDs will not light up when in bootloader mode.

Changelog

1.2.6.7 Beta (1.3RC) (2026-11-16)

  • New: Compressor or Tape Lo-Fi modes can be enabled on any of the 9 Models!
    You can override the default LO-FI types on each model by holding ALT and simultaneously moving the position of the LO-FI Switch.
    • (OFF) Default Per-Bank LO-FI Effects
    • (MIN) LO-FI Tape Effect on all banks (ALT LED will fade slowly in PINK)
    • (MAX) LO-FI Compressor effect on all banks (ALT LED will fade slowly in PURPLE)
    • When in Bank Z, and in LO-FI OVERRIDE mode, you can hold ALT+BURST+LO-FI Sw to change the underlying Model's LO-FI setting.
  • New: INPUT (Knob)/DRIVE position (like Sealegs)
    This changes the signal chain position of the INPUT knob, matching a similar option in Sealegs. Note that Swells has a DRIVE toggle, which is a bit different than Sealegs. Toggle by holding ALT and changing the (IN/SC/OUT) Switch, the middle position is the factory default.
    • When DRIVE is disabled—
      • (IN) Input Level (Input Activity LED will be PINK with activity)
      • (SC) Verb Input (Input/Mix Activity LED will be GREEN with activity)
      • (OUT) Clean Mix Boost into Compressor (Mix Activity LED will be PINK with activity)
    • When DRIVE is enabled (matching Sealegs)—
      • (IN) Input Drive (Input Activity LED will be PINK with activity)
      • (SC) Verb Input Drive (Input/Mix Activity LED will be GREEN with activity)
      • (OUT) Mix Drive into output compressor (Mix Activity LED will be PINK with activity)
  • Tweak: TRIG inputs will now Flip (instead of OR) the various toggle settings.
  • Fixed: Cleaner noise floor.
  • Fixed: CV Calibration not being applied correctly. (Regression)
  • Note: These new ALT+Switch functions require you to hold ALT while the switch changes position, unlike the other panel-labelled Alt functions. More details will be available in the manual when this release is out of beta.
