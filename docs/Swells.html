Swells

Entering Bootloader

  1. Turn off power to your modular system.
  2. Connect a USB cable from the rear of the module to your computer.
  3. Hold down the BOOT button on the rear of the module and turn on power to your modular system.

    • LPF→EF shown on MIX Activity LED
    • HPF→VERB shown on MIX-LEVEL LED

  • Tweak: Improved Swell One Shot/Delayed One Shot/Looper signal handling.
  • Fixed: THRESH knob now works on the Alt page.
  • Fixed: LPF→EF behaviours.
  • Fixed: Trigger behaviour on One Shot Swell mode (GREEN).
  • Fixed: X3 audio glitches when FLOW was set to max.
  • Minor Bug Fixes and Tweaks.
  • Updated manual available at intellijel.com/support.
