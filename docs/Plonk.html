Plonk

Entering Bootloader

  1. Turn off power to your modular system.
  2. Connect a USB cable from the rear of the module to your computer.
  3. Hold down the button on the rear of the module and turn on power to your modular system.

Changelog

1.16 (2019-05-09)

  • BUGFIX: Fix for when Velocity is assigned to "Volume".

1.15 (2019-01-07)

  • Increased range of output gain. Renamed parameter from "Gain" to "Output gain".

1.14 (2018-12-25)

  • FEATURE: Saturator effect, accessible from the CONFIG menu and as a mod destination.
  • FEATURE: Bitcrush effect, accessible from the CONFIG menu and as a mod destination.

1.13 (2018-9-12)

  • BUGFIX: Fix noise filter envelope parameters.

1.12 (2018-9-11)

  • Update to support a new hardware revision. Users of 1.11 on existing modules do not need to install this update.

1.11 (2018-6-15)

  • BUGFIX: recalibration no longer required after update.

1.10 (2018-5-11)

  • FEATURE: New parameters: Noise Lowpass Envelope, Noise Highpass Envelope, control the amount of the noise envelope applied to the lowpass and highpass filters.
  • FEATURE: New x/y/mod destinations: N LP Env, N HP Env, R Pitch
  • FEATURE: Less frequently adjusted global options moved to CONFIG->Global Config sub menu.
  • FEATURE: New global option: Gate delay. Adjustable gate delay for sequencers that don't stabilize pitch until some time after the gate goes high. Adjusting this helps Plonk stay in tune when being controlled by those sources. Lower values will result in lower latency, down to ~2ms. Higher values will increase the latency.
  • FEATURE: Improved overall pitch tracking.
  • BUGFIX: Other minor fixes.

1.00 (2017-7-20)

  • Initial release.
