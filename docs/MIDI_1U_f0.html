MIDI 1U (rev001)

Note: Check the back of your module under the MAIN label for the module revision number.

Entering Bootloader

  1. Turn off power to your modular system.
  2. Disconnect all cables, including patch cables and DIN MIDI cables from the MIDI 1U.
  3. Connect a USB cable from the MIDI 1U module to your computer.
  4. Hold down the LEARN button on the module and turn on power to your modular system.
