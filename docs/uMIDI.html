µMIDI

Entering Bootloader

  1. Turn off power to your modular system.
  2. Disconnect all cables, including patch cables and DIN MIDI cables from the µMIDI.
  3. Connect a USB cable from the µMIDI module to your computer.
  4. Hold down the LEARN button on the module and turn on power to your modular system.
