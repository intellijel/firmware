Entering Bootloader

  1. Turn off power to your modular system.
  2. Connect a USB cable from the rear of the module to your computer.
  3. Hold down the LOAD button on the front panel and turn on power to your modular system.

Update Instructions

  1. From the version menu above, select the firmware version you will be updating to.
  2. Click the Update button in the bottom of this window. You will see the progress of the update in the log window and your module may begin to flash. You will see an “Update completed successfully” message at the end of the update.
  3. Turn your modular off and back on again, you're now ready to use your updated module.
  4. If there are any problems please contact us via the Help button on our website. Be sure to include the full log window output, the firmware updater version downloaded, and the OS you are using.
